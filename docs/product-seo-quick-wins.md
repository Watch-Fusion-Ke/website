## Delivery wording

The contact page says Nairobi delivery carries a location-dependent fee. For delivery outside Nairobi it asks customers to message their location on WhatsApp to confirm what is possible. Some catalog SEO says "Delivery across Kenya", but the more specific contact-page wording supports: "Nairobi delivery; elsewhere in Kenya, ask on WhatsApp."

The new copy makes no promise about free delivery, delivery times, or nationwide availability.

## SEO behavior

These four exact slugs use the reviewed copy ahead of catalog SEO, including HM-CZ32's existing title and description. All other products retain the existing Wix-first behavior. Prices in titles and descriptions use the same current product price supplied to the offer markup, so later catalog price changes do not leave a fixed SEO price behind. A missing, non-finite or non-positive price retains the existing catalog or description fallback.

Titles retain the shop-name suffix and the absolute-title convention. Search metadata and social previews use the same title and description; product structured data uses that description too. Canonical URLs, images, product names and availability retain their existing behavior.

The brand category pages are outside this product-page change. Their search performance remains relevant to the report's broader brand-query work.
